# Name Ideas

## Overview
Brainstormed name candidates for the asteroid survival game, generated from the core concept in [Game Planning for Non-Programmers](game-planning-for-non-programmers.md) (one-hit-death, Flappy Bird-style addictive restart loop).

## Key Points

### Short & punchy (Flappy Bird energy)
- Rock Dodger
- Asteroid Dash
- One Hit
- Space Splat
- Dodge & Blast

### Emphasizing the "survive" tension
- Survive the Belt
- Last Ship Standing
- Hailstorm (asteroids raining in from all sides)
- No Second Chances
- Endless Belt

### Playful/punny
- Roid Rage
- Asteroids? Never Heard of 'Em
- Space Rocks Fall
- Stone Cold Space
- Meteor Meltdown

### Top pick
For a mobile hyper-casual audience: **Rock Dodger** — short, googleable, hints at the core loop without over-explaining it.
